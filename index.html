Shelby is a co-founder of Abide House Columbus and the artist behind Shelby Lane Beauty. She specializes in soft, lived-in blonde, dimensional color, and seamless hand-tied extensions — every appointment personalized, never rushed.

View Services & Book Call or Text

About Shelby

Color is her craft.

With a focus on blonding and dimensional color, Shelby takes the time to understand your hair, your routine, and your goals before she ever picks up a brush — so your color looks effortless and grows out beautifully.

She’s also an extension specialist, creating natural length and fullness with hand-tied wefts.

Specialties

  • Dimensional & All-Over Color
  • Hand-Tied Weft Extensions
  • Color Correction & Transformations
  • Cuts & Special-Occasion Styling
